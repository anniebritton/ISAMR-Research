# Future Work

Ideas carried over from the "Data Ideas" cell of the original notebook, plus directions raised by the current results.

## From the Original Notebook

* Hudson Bay sea ice.
  Break-up timing may cool the coastal strip in spring and delay green-up near the shore.
  Candidate data: NSIDC sea-ice concentration or the Canadian Ice Service regional charts.
* Albedo.
  MCD43A3 could link snow and surface energy balance to the timing of melt.
* The local weather station at Wapusk or Churchill Northern Studies Centre, for predictors measured inside or next to the park.
* [Prairie Climate Centre](https://prairieclimatecentre.ca/about-us/) projections, to model green-up under future climate.
* Predicting when caribou arrive from climate and phenology variables.
  This needs caribou observation or collar data.

## Raised by the Results

* Extend the record and increase sensitivity with Landsat and Sentinel-2 (HLS, 2013 onward) at 30 m.
* Ground truth: Churchill Northern Studies Centre phenology plots, the long-term goose research at La Pérouse Bay,
  and any PhenoCam sites near Churchill.
* Test the effect of Terra's orbit drift on 2022–2024 by comparing Terra and Aqua (MYD09GA).
* Spatial pattern: a per-pixel trend and driver map, which the fixed sample supports only coarsely.
